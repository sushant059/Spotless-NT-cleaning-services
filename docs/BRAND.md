# Spotless NT brand guidelines

Short rules so the website, uniforms, vehicle, socials and print all look like one business.

## Logo

- The mark is the house, vacuum, sparkles and green swoosh (`public/spotless-nt-mark.png`).
- Wordmark: **Spotless** in navy, **NT** in green, with **CLEANING SERVICES** underneath in small spaced capitals.
- Always place the mark on white or on a white rounded tile. On dark backgrounds use the white tile (as in the footer).
- Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "S".
- Don't stretch, recolour, add shadows to, or rotate the mark.

## Colours

Every colour is sampled from the logo.

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Territory Navy | `#0B2F5E` | Headings, dark sections, uniforms |
| Navy Deep | `#061C3A` | Footer, darkest backgrounds |
| Spotless Blue | `#0067B1` | Links, icons, secondary buttons |
| Sparkle Sky | `#2E9BEA` | Highlights, sparkles, accents on dark |
| Fresh Leaf | `#3BA935` | Swoosh accents, success ticks, dots |
| Leaf Dark | `#24801F` | Main call-to-action buttons (white text) |
| Sun | `#FFB547` | Star ratings and "pending" badges only |
| Mist | `#F3F7FC` | Light section backgrounds |
| Ink | `#0C1B2F` | Body text |

Rules:
- The main action ("Get a free quote") is always **Leaf Dark with white text**.
- Use white text only on Navy, Navy Deep, Spotless Blue and Leaf Dark (all pass WCAG AA).
- Sun is for stars and small badges. Never use it as a background for long text.

## Typography

- **Headings:** Bricolage Grotesque, ExtraBold (800), tight letter spacing.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans, Regular (400) and SemiBold (600).
- Eyebrow labels: 12px, bold, uppercase, wide letter spacing, Spotless Blue, with a small sparkle.

## Signature details

- **The sparkle:** the four-point star from the logo. Use it sparingly as an accent, a bullet or a subtle background shape.
- **The swoosh:** a soft green curve, used as a divider or under a figure.
- Rounded corners everywhere (cards 28px, buttons fully round).
- Navy hero sections with a faint grid and a blue glow.

## Uniform

- Navy polo or tee, blue collar trim, a thin green stripe across the chest.
- Front: the logo in a white circle on the left chest.
- Back: a large logo, **SPOTLESS NT** and **CLEANING SERVICES** in white, and the phone number in green.
- Cap: navy with a white sparkle badge.
- The About page shows a front-and-back mockup you can give to a printer.

## Voice

- Plain, friendly and honest. Australian spelling.
- Say what a clean can and can't do. Never promise a stain will vanish or a bond will be returned.
- Use real numbers only (no made-up "500+ happy customers").

## Photos & video

- Shoot in natural light, on a phone in landscape for before/after and portrait for TikTok/Reels.
- Before and after should be the same angle and distance.
- Show the uniform and the logo in team photos.
- Upload them in Admin → Services (per-service gallery and video), Before & after, and Gallery & media (hero and team photos).
